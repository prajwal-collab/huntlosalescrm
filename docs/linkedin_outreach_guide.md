# SDR Guide: LinkedIn Outreach Module

Welcome to the new LinkedIn Outreach tracking module! We've built this to make logging your LinkedIn activity as fast as possible, while automatically syncing everything to the main CRM so no leads fall through the cracks.

Here’s how to use the new features.

---

## 1. The LinkedIn Hub
You'll see a new **LinkedIn** tab in the main sidebar. This is your central command center for all LinkedIn activity.

### The Rapid Logger
Use the Quick Logger at the top of the hub to log new touches instantly:
1. **Paste the LinkedIn URL**: The system will automatically check if this lead already exists. If it does, it will auto-fill their name and company!
2. **Fill Details**: Ensure Name, Company, and Action Type are filled out.
3. **Log Sentiment**: If they replied, make sure to mark the sentiment (e.g., Interested, Demo Booked, Not Interested).
4. **Hit `Cmd+Enter`**: Instantly logs the touch and syncs it.

> **💡 Magic Sync**: If the lead didn't exist in the CRM, the logger will **automatically create a new Lead, Company, and Contact** for you. If they did exist, it adds a touch to their timeline and updates their stage.

### CSV Import (Bulk Logging)
If you do bulk outreach via Sales Navigator or Apollo:
1. Click **Import CSV** in the top right.
2. Drop your CSV file (make sure it has Name, Company, and LinkedIn URL columns).
3. The system will process the list, log the touches, and create/update all leads in seconds.

---

## 2. Lead Drawer Quick Logger
You don't need to leave a lead's profile to log a LinkedIn touch!

When you click on a Lead in the CRM to open the right-side drawer:
1. Go to the **Outreach** tab.
2. At the bottom, you'll see the **🔗 Quick LinkedIn Logger**.
3. Select your action, add a note, and hit **Log**. It will instantly update the lead's history.

---

## 3. LinkedIn Smart View
On the main **Leads** page, you can now filter your leads by LinkedIn activity.
- Click on the **🔗 LinkedIn Outreach** smart view at the top of the Leads table to see every prospect that you've touched via LinkedIn.

---

## 4. How Your Metrics Are Tracked
Your LinkedIn activity now counts directly towards your KPIs!
- The **Admin Dashboard** and **Reports** tab will show your total LinkedIn Touches and LinkedIn Replies.
- Replying leads marked as "Interested" or "Demo Booked" will automatically move the lead to the "Qualified" or "Demo Scheduled" pipeline stages for you.

*Happy Hunting!*
